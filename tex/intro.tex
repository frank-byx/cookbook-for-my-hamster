\section*{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{\nameref{sec:intro}}

This is a cuisine designed for my hamster. The main constraints on the design of this cuisine are the low frequency of meals and my hamster's preferences.
Thus, the components are chosen with long-term storage in mind, and individual dishes are kept simple, to be combined for meals, optimizing the usage of perishable ingredients,
which are kept to those that my hamster particularly enjoys.
