\recdef{Shrimp Aroma Oil}
Extraction of spot prawn heads and shells with ginger in peanut oil.
When combined with \recref{Scallion Oil} in a 4:1 ratio,
the resulting ratio of scallions:garlic:ginger is 4:1:2.

\begin{rec}
\ing{15 (300 g)}{spot prawn heads and shells}
Pat dry and peel heads to expose innards.
\ing{300 g}{peanut oil (Lion \& Globe ``peanut oil for seafood'')}
\ing{15 g}{fresh ginger, skin on, thinly sliced}
Gently fry prawn heads and shells with ginger in oil while stirring until shells turn golden and aromatics are fragrant.
Let cool to room temperature.
\newstep
Strain oil and discard solids. Store in freezer.
\end{rec}
