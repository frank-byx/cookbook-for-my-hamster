\recdef{Shrimp Aroma Oil}
Extraction of spot prawn heads and shells in peanut oil.

\begin{rec}
\ing{10}{spot prawn heads and shells}
Pat dry and peel heads to expose innards.
\ing{300 g}{peanut oil (Lion \& Globe ``peanut oil for seafood'')}
Gently fry in oil while stirring until shells turn golden. Cool to room temperature.
\newstep
Strain oil and discard solids. Store in freezer.
\end{rec}
