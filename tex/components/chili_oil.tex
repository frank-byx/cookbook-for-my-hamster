\recdef{Chili Oil}
Extraction of dried chilis in roasted rapeseed oil.

\begin{rec}
\ing{90 g}{Er Jing Tiao chilis}
\ing{60 g}{Chao Tian Jiao chilis}
Toast on low heat until fragrant.
De-seed chilis and blitz into fine powder.
\ing{10 g}{smoked paprika}
Mix in smoked paprika.
\ing{300 g}{Roasted Rapeseed Oil}
Heat oil to to 190$^\circ$C.
Arrange chili powder into volcano shape.
\newstep
Pour half of oil and mix well.
\newstep
Let oil cool to 135$^\circ$C. Pour half of remaining oil and mix well.
\newstep
Let oil cool to 85$^\circ$C. Pour remaining oil and mix well.
\newstep
Let oil cool to room temperature and refrigerate for 1 day. Strain and discard solids. Store in freezer.
\end{rec}
