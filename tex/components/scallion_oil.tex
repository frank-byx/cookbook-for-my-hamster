\recdef{Scallion Oil}
Extraction of scallions with garlic and ginger in neutral oil, in a 4:1:1 ratio.

\begin{rec}
\ing{300 g}{grapeseed oil}
Heat oil to 160$^\circ$C.
\ing{120 g}{scallion whites, cut into 2-inch pieces}
\ing{60 g}{garlic, germ removed, sliced}
\ing{60 g}{fresh ginger, skin on, thinly sliced}
Simmer in oil until garlic slices are golden and scallion whites are dark brown.
Remove the solids.
\newstep
\ing{120 g}{scallion greens, thinly sliced}
Remove oil from heat and stir in scallion greens.
Let cool to room temperature and steep refrigerated overnight.
\newstep
Strain oil and discard solids. Store in freezer.
\end{rec}
