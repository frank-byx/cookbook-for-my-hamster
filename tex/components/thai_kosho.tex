\recdef{Thai Kosho}
Aged salted paste of red Thai chilis and lime zest.

\begin{rec}
\ing{100 g}{fresh red Thai chilis, finely minced}
\ing{15 g}{salt}
Pound minced chilis with salt into a paste with a mortar and pestle.
\ing{50 g}{lime zest, microplaned}
Add lime zest and pound until combined.
\newstep
Vacuum seal and store refrigerated for 2-3 weeks.
\end{rec}
