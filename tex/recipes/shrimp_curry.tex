\recdef{Shrimp Curry}
Are you telling me a shrimp made this curry?

\begin{rec}
\ing{10 (160 g)}{spot prawn tails, peeled}
\ing{0.8 g}{baking soda}
\ing{0.8 g}{salt}
Toss prawns in baking soda and salt. Refrigerate for 30 minutes.
Rinse and pat dry.
\ing{10 g}{\recref{Thai Kosho}}
\ing{20 g}{\recref{Shrimp Aroma Oil}}
Whisk together and toss with prawns.
Vacuum seal and sous vide at 55$^\circ$C for 15 mins.
\ing{5 g}{\recref{Thai Kosho}}
\ing{20 g}{\recref{Shrimp Aroma Oil}}
\ing{10 g}{\recref{Spiced Ghee}}
\ing{10 g}{\recref{Chili Oil}}
\ing{10 g}{\recref{Trinity Oil}}
Combine in saucepan and fry for 1 min until fragrant.
\ing{100 g}{\recref{Kabocha Puree}}
Add kabocha puree and stir to combine. Fry until lightly caramelized and oil begins to separate.
\newstep
\ing{150 g}{coconut milk}
\ing{10 g}{soy sauce}
\ing{5 g}{\recref{Frozen Lime Juice}}
Remove pan from heat. Whisk in liquids.
Then, stir in the entire contents of the hot vacuum bag with prawns.
\newstep
\ing{10 g}{\recref{Cilantro Oil}}
Transfer to pre-warmed bowl and garnish with cilantro oil. Serve immediately.
\end{rec}
